## Summary

Positioning is commonly misunderstood and distinct from messaging, branding, or taglines. According to Dunford, positioning should define how a product is a leader at delivering something that a well-defined set of customers cares a lot about, establishing crucial context that shapes customer expectations about market category, features, and competitors. Effective positioning requires thoughtfully defining five key components: competitive alternatives, differentiated capabilities, customer value, target segmentation, and market category, rather than relying on traditional positioning statement templates that oversimplify strategic decisions.
