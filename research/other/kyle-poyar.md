## Summary

Kyle Poyar shares 25 tactics for growing a B2B newsletter based on five years of experience growing Growth Unhinged to over 80,000 subscribers. He emphasizes foundational elements like choosing a memorable name, securing a custom domain, and establishing a consistent visual identity and publishing cadence. The article advises focusing on "singles and doubles" through tactical, specific content rather than viral home runs, noting that regular posts generating 50-150 subscribers each account for 55% of total growth compared to just 14% from top viral posts.
