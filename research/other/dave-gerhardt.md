## Summary

Dave Gerhardt shares his LinkedIn strategy, explaining that he keeps ideas in Apple Notes, writes between 6-9am, and posts almost daily while prioritizing authentic writing with typos and casual formatting to stand out amid AI-generated content. He emphasizes that success comes from doubling down on his strengths instead of trying to round himself out, focusing on becoming exceptional in one area rather than mediocre across many. The piece also covers his broader philosophy on avoiding excessive seriousness, maintaining work-life balance with zero-meeting days when possible, and being genuinely honest about personal struggles and motivations rather than just showcasing wins.
