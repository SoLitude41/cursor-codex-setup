## Summary

Amanda Natividad discusses Jay Acunzo's perspective that effective communication should prioritize resonance and genuine value over reach and marketing volume. The core argument emphasizes that speakers and creators should focus on being so good that the seeking-out happens on its own, measuring success through unsolicited responses rather than traffic metrics. Acunzo advocates for meeting audiences where they are by naming problems and building context before offering solutions, rather than rushing to deliver answers.
