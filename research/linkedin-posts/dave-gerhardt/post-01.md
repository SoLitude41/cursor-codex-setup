You know what is incredibly hard to do? Run a great business.



I don't think I appreciated it until starting my company, but I look at every business differently now - from the breakfast place down the street to ordering a new piece of gym equipment to an enterprise cyber security company.



It is freaking HARD to hire the right people, train, build a culture, find product market fit, set the right expectations, be able to service customers in the right way, handle feedback, change the menu, raise prices, keep the lights on, organize the books, figure out how to pay and get paid, taxes IT IS A LOT and then you have to find ways to stay relevant with AI, stay up to speed on AI, all the things.



To build a reliable product/service is no small feat.



And I think my business is relatively simple and easy to operate.



Much respect to the Founders and Business Owners. 



The past 4 years running Exit Five has shed light on so many things I did not see before. 



It's kind of like being a parent. Once you have a kid you realize why your parents did things the way they did.
