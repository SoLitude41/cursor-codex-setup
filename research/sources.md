# Sources

Research topic: LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS

## Collection Methodology

1. Use the LinkedIn profile links as expert identity anchors.
2. Use the LinkedIn post links as primary examples of organic LinkedIn content.
3. Use the YouTube links as transcript candidates.
4. Do not paste or summarize transcripts until the transcript is manually confirmed from YouTube captions or another legitimate transcript source.
5. Store LinkedIn post notes in `research/linkedin-posts/`.
6. Store YouTube transcript candidates and confirmed transcripts in `research/youtube-transcripts/`.
7. Store non-LinkedIn/non-YouTube reference material in `research/other/`.
