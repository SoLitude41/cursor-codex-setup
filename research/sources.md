# Sources

Research topic: LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS

## Collection Methodology

1. Use LinkedIn profile links as expert identity anchors.
2. Use LinkedIn post links as primary examples of organic LinkedIn content.
3. Use YouTube links as transcript candidates.
4. Record dates for when sources were added to this repository.
5. Do not paste or summarize transcripts until the transcript is manually confirmed from YouTube captions or another legitimate transcript source.
6. Store LinkedIn post notes by author in `research/linkedin-posts/`.
7. Store YouTube transcript candidates and confirmed transcripts by video in `research/youtube-transcripts/`.
8. Store non-LinkedIn/non-YouTube reference material in `research/other/`.
